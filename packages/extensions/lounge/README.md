# Axl Lounge extension

This package owns first-party Lounge activities. It contains deterministic Wordle, 2048, Minesweeper, Sudoku, and Chess Puzzle rule engines and exposes all five games through the public extension API. Wordle uses reviewed offline dictionaries. Its internal identifiers retain `codeword` for save compatibility.

The engine exports immutable state reducers, duplicate-aware scoring, normal and hard mode validation, six-attempt completion, restart behavior, and versioned daily and practice selection. Callers supply the UTC puzzle date or practice seed. The engine never reads the system clock or global randomness. The activity renders semantic frames and owns only game-local input, help, mode selection, restart confirmation, and bounded activity-storage state.

Codeword saves retain the dictionary revision, selection-algorithm version, UTC puzzle date or practice seed, exact board state, mode preferences, completion keys, and local aggregate statistics. Active boards use storage compare-and-swap and never merge implicitly. Completion records may merge by stable key after a concurrent update.

Submitted rows reveal from left to right at 80 ms per tile. Reduced-motion and text-only presentation preferences apply the complete result immediately. Text-only frames keep metadata, status, numbered board rows, and keyboard evidence in stable reading order. Decorative reveal and win-emphasis callbacks are cancelled on pause, focus loss, undersize, and disposal. The normal presentation uses centered box-drawing tiles, a fixed-width keyboard, and prominent result cards. The host keeps the live agent transcript beside the game on wide terminals, lets `Tab` switch panes without pausing this turn-based game, and leaves Wordle open behind a visible agent-completion notice.

2048 uses a pure 4×4 reducer with versioned deterministic spawning, one merge per tile per move, exact one-move undo, win continuation, game-over detection, and strict resumable storage. Arrow, WASD, and HJKL movement share one move calculation and trace, including exact source, destination, merge, landed board, and spawned cell. The board uses sixteen separate allocation-sized boxes with large centered values. Rounded, square, double, and heavy outlines create a restrained value hierarchy that remains clear without color. A move immediately shows the landed board without its new tile; after 67 ms the final board adds the new tile with a dotted outline while merged destinations retain a heavy outline. There are no coordinate labels or simulated sliding frames. At most one movement key is queued, with no catch-up burst. Reduced-motion and text-only modes apply the final board immediately and schedule no decorative callback. `U` is available for exactly one move, `R` restarts with confirmation after active progress, `C` continues beyond 2048, and `?` opens in-pane help.

Minesweeper delays deterministic mine placement until the first reveal. The first cell is safe, and its neighbors are safe for every included preset. Beginner is 9×9 with 10 mines, Intermediate is 16×16 with 40, and Expert is 30×16 with 99. Arrows or HJKL move the cursor, Space or Enter reveals a hidden cell or chords a satisfied number, F flags, and C explicitly chords. Left-click follows the same reveal-or-chord behavior, right-click toggles a flag, and middle-click chords. Touch works only when the terminal client translates taps into mouse events; Axl does not implement native touch gestures. Large boards use a cursor-following clipped viewport with fixed coordinate gutters and horizontal and vertical axis separators. A contextual inspector explains the selected cell and calls out chord-ready numbers. The display distinguishes hidden cells, flags, open cells, numbers, mines, explosions, incorrect flags, and the cursor without relying on color. Win and loss keep the exposed board visible beneath a stable result banner and direct next actions. Its active-play timer uses the host monotonic clock and stops whenever the game is hidden, unfocused, suspended, undersized, or complete. Saves retain the exact logical board, cursor, PRNG state, and elapsed active time.

Sudoku ships twelve immutable, versioned, independently generated puzzles across Easy, Medium, and Hard. Every puzzle is regenerated deterministically and verified to have exactly one solution. Heavy outer and 3×3 rules, thin individual-cell rules at common terminal heights, and shaded peer cells keep the traditional Sudoku structure stable at every supported width. Arrows or HJKL move, digits enter, Backspace or Delete erases, N toggles notes, U undoes, G opens a hint confirmation, D changes difficulty, and R starts a new puzzle. A confirmed hint fills the first empty row-major cell from the verified solution and remains undoable. Fixed cell markers distinguish selected cells, peers, givens, entries, notes, hints, and every conflicting duplicate without relying on color. Saves contain only exact logical state and undo history.

Chess Puzzles ships a reviewed offline set of 1,000 CC0 Lichess tactics across Easy, Medium, and Hard from revision `lichess-2026-09-10-axl-chess-v1`. Daily and Practice selection is deterministic from injected UTC dates and seeds. The board starts from the player's side and can be flipped. Arrows or HJKL move in visual directions. Enter, Space, and left click share source and destination handling. Fixed markers distinguish the cursor, selected source, legal moves, captures, setup and last moves, check, and progressive hints without relying on color. Promotion requires an explicit Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight choice. Correct player moves appear immediately, while the exact sourced opponent reply is scheduled separately unless text-only or reduced-motion presentation requests an immediate reply. The final board remains visible with a `PUZZLE SOLVED` result and next actions. Difficulty, reviewed theme, Daily or Practice mode, hints, help, restart, and board flip remain available in-pane. Saves reconstruct progress from the canonical line and keep bounded completion statistics.

With more than one registered game, `Ctrl+P` opens the game library. Wide layouts keep the live Agent transcript and composer on the left and place the library on the right. `Tab` changes focus when Agent-local completion does not own it. Returning to the current game resumes the same activity instance and preserves its state.
